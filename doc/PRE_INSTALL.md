Spoolman has **no login of its own**: anyone who can reach it can read and edit your inventory. Access is therefore controlled entirely by YunoHost permissions, and the app is private (all YunoHost users) by default.

Printer software such as Klipper/Moonraker or OctoPrint cannot log in through the YunoHost portal. If you want to connect one of them, see the admin documentation after installation.
